**Topic:** A common AI or marketing mistake small businesses make — and an easy fix

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Feeling overwhelmed by all the AI tools out there for marketing? You're not alone. A common mistake we see small businesses make is trying to implement too many AI solutions at once, or not knowing where to begin, which just adds to your already full plate.

The fix? Think 'augmentation' over 'automation' to start. Pick *one* specific marketing task that takes up a lot of your team's time – maybe it's brainstorming blog topics, drafting initial social media captions, or summarizing customer feedback. 💡 Then, find an AI tool that can assist with *that single task*.

By starting small and focused, you'll see tangible benefits quickly without the burnout. AI becomes a helpful co-pilot, not another source of stress. What's one marketing task you'd love a little AI help with?
