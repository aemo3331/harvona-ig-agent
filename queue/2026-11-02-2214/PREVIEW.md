**Topic:** What we wish every founder knew before spending money on marketing

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Ever wondered if your marketing budget is truly working, or just… existing? 

Before you spend a single dollar, take a breath. The biggest marketing mistake isn't spending too little or too much; it's spending without a clear strategy. 💡 You need to know *who* you're talking to and *what* you want them to do.

Think of it like building a house. You wouldn't buy lumber before seeing the blueprints, right? Your marketing strategy is your blueprint. What are your core goals? Who is your ideal customer? What specific problem are you solving for them? Answering these questions first makes every dollar you spend so much more effective.

Even with a lean team, you can lay this foundation. It doesn't have to be complicated. Getting clear on your strategy *before* the spend saves you time, money, and frustration down the line. What's the best marketing advice you ever received?
