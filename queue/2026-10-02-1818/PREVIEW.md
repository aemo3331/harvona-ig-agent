**Topic:** A simple weekly marketing routine a busy founder can actually stick to

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Ever feel like marketing is a never-ending to-do list? You're not alone. As a founder, your plate is already full, but consistent marketing doesn't have to mean constant stress. It's about building small, manageable habits that add up to big results. 📈

Imagine dedicating just 30-60 minutes each week to marketing. That could look like: a quick check-in on your best-performing content, drafting one social media post, or outlining a quick email to your list. The secret isn't grand gestures, it's showing up regularly and making small, intentional steps.

This focused approach helps you stay top-of-mind with your audience and gather valuable insights without overwhelming your lean team. Plus, with the right tools, you can automate or streamline much of the heavy lifting. Save this post for your next planning session!
