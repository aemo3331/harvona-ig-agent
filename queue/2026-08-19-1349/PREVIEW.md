**Topic:** What good marketing results actually look like for a small business, and how to measure them

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Ever feel overwhelmed by marketing numbers, wondering if what you're seeing is actually "good"? It's easy to get lost in vanity metrics, but for small businesses and founders, good results directly connect to your business goals. 🎯

Good marketing means more than just likes. It could be a steady increase in qualified leads, higher conversion rates on your website, more repeat customers, or a growing email list that converts. Think about what truly moves the needle for *your* bottom line, then track the metrics that reflect those goals. AI tools can help your lean team quickly identify patterns and focus on what truly works.

Instead of chasing every trend, focus on consistent effort and clear measurement tied to your unique business objectives. It makes marketing less of a mystery and more of a predictable growth engine. What's one marketing metric you're excited to improve this quarter?
