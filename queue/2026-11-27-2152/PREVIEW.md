**Topic:** A common AI or marketing mistake small businesses make — and an easy fix

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Ever feel like your AI tools aren't quite hitting the mark? Maybe the content feels a little generic, or the suggestions aren't truly 'you'? A common mistake we see small businesses make is treating AI like a magic button. 

It's easy to just type a quick prompt and hope for the best, but that often leads to 'meh' results. Think of AI as a brilliant assistant who needs clear, specific instructions. If you give it vague input, it'll give you vague output – and that's a waste of your valuable time and resources.

The fix is simple: level up your prompts! Tell your AI tool about your target audience, your specific goal, and your brand's unique voice. Provide examples if you can. The more context you give, the more tailored and effective your AI-powered marketing will be. ✨
