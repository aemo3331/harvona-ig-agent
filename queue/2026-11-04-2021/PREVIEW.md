**Topic:** A simple weekly marketing routine a busy founder can actually stick to

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Does your marketing to-do list feel endless? As a busy founder, it’s easy to let marketing slide when you’re juggling everything else. But consistent effort, even small amounts, is what truly moves the needle. 

Instead of grand plans, try this simple weekly routine: Dedicate 30 minutes to review your audience insights (what's working?), 30 minutes to brainstorm new content ideas, and 1 hour to create or schedule one piece of content. That's just two hours total! ⏰ 

It’s not about doing everything; it’s about doing *something* consistently. This small, focused habit builds momentum and ensures your brand stays visible without overwhelming your lean team.
