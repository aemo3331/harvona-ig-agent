**Topic:** How to write marketing copy faster with AI without it sounding robotic

![image](./image.jpg)

## Caption

Staring at a blank page, trying to conjure the perfect marketing copy? We've all been there! AI can be a huge time-saver, but nobody wants their brand to sound like a robot. 🤖

The secret to great AI-assisted copy is starting with your unique brand voice. Don't just ask AI to 'write a social post.' Instead, prompt it with *your* specific tone, audience, and key messages. Think of AI as your super-fast first draft assistant, not the final word.

Always, always give it a human edit. Tweak phrases, add a touch of your personality, and make sure it genuinely sounds like *you*. This keeps your brand authentic while still speeding up your workflow. What's your favorite tip for making AI sound more human?
